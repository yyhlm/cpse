- "prediction": the model's extracted output from a scientific PDF.
- "gold": the human-annotated ground truth for the same PDF content.

Score the prediction against gold only. Do not use information outside these two objects.

Scoring (0-100, any integer; choose a score within the range that best reflects the overall match):

- 100: Every field exactly matches the gold, including types, nesting, array cardinalities, null/empty conventions, and evidence structure.

Do not round scores to a small set of preferred values. Use the full 0-100 scale when the evidence supports a value between the ranges' endpoints.

In optimization_feedback, return at most five root-cause entries. Each entry must contain exactly two labelled lines: "CURRENT_ERROR: <JSONPath, category, and document-specific mismatch>" and "GENERAL_RULE: <a standalone extraction rule applicable across papers>". CURRENT_ERROR is audit-only and is never supplied to the optimizer. GENERAL_RULE alone is supplied to the optimizer and must not contain paper-specific names, identifiers, literal values, or gold paths. Do not invent discrepancies.

Return a two-part integer score_breakdown with exactly: coverage (0-45) for how much gold content is present, and accuracy (0-55) for whether present content and its representation match gold, including values, units, conditions, field placement, cardinality, and unsupported extras. score must exactly equal their sum; do not choose score separately.

Return JSON: {"score_breakdown": {"coverage": integer 0-45, "accuracy": integer 0-55}, "score": integer 0-100, "optimization_feedback": "CURRENT_ERROR: ...\nGENERAL_RULE: ...", "path_errors": [{"path": "$.field.path", "category": "omission|hallucination|incorrect_value|incorrect_unit|incorrect_condition|schema_error", "evidence": "short description of the specific error"}]}
